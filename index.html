About

Before university, I was a professional competitive gamer. I reached the highest level of my game, competing in Japan and living in South Korea, where tournaments were my full-time job. Playing on international teams taught me to adapt to different cultures and personalities, and to work through pressure and conflict.

A game made by strangers on another continent shaped who I became and opened doors I never imagined. Now I want to be on the other side: building games and software that impact someone else the way they impacted me.

That’s what brought me to Singapore Institute of Technology's Computer Science in Real-Time Interactive Simulation. I bring the same teamwork to every project: aligning on goals early, raising misunderstandings before they grow, and staying until the work is done.
